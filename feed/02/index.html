01  The big scroll 4 min read

Skincare gets clinical

The glow era isn’t over, but TikTok searchers are trading aesthetics for actives. “Japanese skincare” is up 271% in three months while “Korean skincare” is down 31%.

The search terms moving fastest are ingredient and regimen words: “retinoid” (+147% in three months, +63% year on year), PDRN (+83% YoY) and “aging” (+44%). Broad aesthetic terms like “glass skin” (−34%) are cooling. For brands, the brief is shifting from “show me the glow” to “prove the formula”: explainers, expert voices and visible results.

J-beauty is the new K-beauty

“Japanese skincare” searches are up 271% in three months and 110% year on year, while “Korean skincare” is down 55% YoY. The next wave is quieter, more minimal routines.

02

Ingredients

PDRN and retinoids go mainstream

#pdrn is 7.6× more likely in the skincare conversation than on TikTok overall, and “retinoid” searches have more than doubled. Creators now lead with the label.

03

Commerce

The review is the storefront

Medicube is tagged by 3% of the skincare audience, 301× the TikTok baseline, and “medicube skincare” searches are up 373% YoY. Reviews run through 53% of the conversation.
